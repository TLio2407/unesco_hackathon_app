# Future Development Roadmap — An Tâm Số (UNESCO MIL App)

This document outlines upcoming features, technical enhancements, and accessibility roadmap items for future development iterations.

---

## 1. Warnings & Threat Intelligence Engine
- **Reliable Feeds Integration**: Automated ingestion of real-time fraud alerts from official databases (Ministry of Public Security, Information Security Authority, State Bank of Vietnam).
- **Network Circle Alert Dispatch**: Push notifications sent automatically to a user's Trusted Circle when a high-risk scam or suspicious URL targeting their demographic is detected.

## 2. Structured Study & Micro-Learning
- **Grouped Lesson Taxonomy**: Organize micro-lessons into structured modules (e.g., *Banking Security*, *Impersonation Recognition*, *E-commerce Safety*, *Personal Data Protection*).
- **Gamified Progress Tracking**: Senior-friendly visual progress badges and achievement certificates upon module completion.

## 3. Responsive Layout & Tablet Optimization
- **Adaptive Grid Breakpoints**: Optimize layout card widths for mid-size screens (tablets, iPads, and large foldable devices).
- **Dual-Pane Views**: Master-detail layout on larger screens for seamless side-by-side reading and verification.

## 4. Accessibility & Senior Inclusion (MIL)
- **Color Blindness Modes**: Dedicated high-contrast color palettes (Protanopia, Deuteranopia, Tritanopia) built into the theme system.
- **Screen Reader Optimization**: Enhanced ARIA tags, `accessibilityLabel`, and focus rings for seamless VoiceOver (iOS) and TalkBack (Android) support.
- **Voice Input & Audio Readback (Text-to-Speech)**: Integrated native voice reading for elderly users who prefer listening over reading long text.

## 5. Multilingual & Regional Dialect Support
- **Dynamic Language Switcher**: Fast toggle between Vietnamese (Standard North, Central, South regional phrasing), English, and ethnic minority languages.
- **Elderly-Friendly Simplified Vocabulary**: Contextual AI translation that replaces technical jargon with simple, clear everyday analogies.
